UsageNotch 2.5 sends your phone a 90-day calendar of usage.

- UsageNotch for Android 1.4 shows it in the History tab as a calendar of weeks, with days that had no readings outlined. Tap a day to see how it compares with your average and what your other limits used.
- The 30 days that Android 1.3 reads are unchanged, so older phone apps keep working.
- Everything is computed on this PC from the history it already keeps. Nothing new is uploaded; with internet sync on, the calendar travels end-to-end encrypted like the rest of your readings.

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates. Your settings, history and phone pairing are kept.
