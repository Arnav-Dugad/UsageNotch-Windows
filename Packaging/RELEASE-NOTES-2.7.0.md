UsageNotch 2.7 understands every kind of account, keeps the dock in front, and makes your stats more accurate and easier to read.

**Every kind of account**
- **ChatGPT Edu, Business and Enterprise seats** show their **Monthly limit** in credits ("30 of 150 credits used") next to the 5-hour and weekly limits, with budgets and alerts like any other limit.
- Your **plan** appears next to each provider: Claude Pro, Max 5× or 20×, Team or Enterprise; ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Edu, Edu Plus or Edu Pro; your Cursor membership; your Gemini tier.
- Codex shows a **credit balance** you bought, an available **free limit reset** (with its use-by date), and why a limit stopped (for example, "Your workspace is out of credits").
- Claude now shows **every limit** your account reports, including dollar-based usage credits, and **where this week went** (for example, Claude Code 100%).

**The dock stays in front**
- Other always-on-top apps could cover the dock. UsageNotch now brings it back to the front whenever another window comes forward, without taking focus.

**More accurate data**
- Usage that happened while your PC was off or asleep is now counted (on the day it was next read), and so is usage right after a limit renews. Before, both were left out of daily totals.
- Small downward wobbles in a provider's reading are no longer mistaken for a reset.
- UsageNotch reads again as soon as your PC wakes or you unlock it, and twice as often while your usage is moving.
- Claude's percentages keep their full precision.

**Easier to read**
- History speaks in limits: under one full limit as a percentage ("48%"), above it as a multiple ("5.6× the limit, 5.6 full session limits' worth").
- Daily bars show a "1 full limit" line, and History says how much of each day this PC actually observed.
- Day cards compare a day with your usual ("1.6× more", "30% less").

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates. UsageNotch for Android 1.7 shows the same plans, monthly limits and clearer stats.
