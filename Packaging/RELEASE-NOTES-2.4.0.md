UsageNotch 2.4 sends more to your phone and makes internet sync a two-click setup.

**On your phone (with UsageNotch for Android 1.3)**
- **History**: 30 days of daily usage, your current streak, and a weekday × hour map of your busiest times, from the history this PC already keeps. Days and hours without readings show as no data, never as zero.
- **Pace**: each limit carries the dock's own forecast, such as "At this pace: limit around 4:10 PM". Android 1.3 can warn you when a limit would run out within two hours of use, before it resets.

**Internet sync in two clicks**
- Choose **Turn on internet sync** in Settings → Phone. GitHub opens with a token already limited to gists. Click **Generate token**, then the copy icon. UsageNotch takes the token from the clipboard, clears the clipboard and turns sync on. It only accepts a token copied after you clicked, and it stops waiting after five minutes.
- Paired phones switch to internet sync by themselves the next time they reach this PC on Wi-Fi. There's no new code to scan. The sync address and key travel only over the pinned HTTPS link to phones that are already paired, and are never uploaded.
- GitHub tokens expire after the time you pick on that page. When one does, UsageNotch says so and you turn sync on again. **Paste a token instead** is still there.

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates. Your settings, history and phone pairing are kept.
