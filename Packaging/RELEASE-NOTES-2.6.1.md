UsageNotch 2.6.1 refines the controls in Stats & Settings.

- **Crisp rounded keys instead of ovals.** Buttons, chips, segmented controls, the tab bar and the status capsule are now rounded rectangles with a specular rim, a soft top sheen and a gentle shadow. The selected tab or segment is a raised glass key.
- **Accent buttons** are filled with your accent, lit slightly brighter at the top.
- **Window controls** stay flat until you point at them, as in Windows 11.
- Expander headers follow the light and dark themes.

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates.
