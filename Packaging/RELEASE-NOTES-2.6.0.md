UsageNotch 2.6 redesigns Stats & Settings in the same liquid glass as UsageNotch for Android, adds budgets, a best time to start, more accounts and your local token use, and keeps your PC and phone in step.

**A new workspace**
- **Overview, History and Settings** on a glass tab bar whose selection glides between tabs. Soft coloured light drifts behind everything, tinted by your providers.
- **Overview**: your rings, open incidents, then a card for every provider with each limit's bar, a live countdown, the pace, your budget and the best time to start.
- **Click a ring or card** and it grows into a full view: a large ring, the pace, the best time to start, budgets for every limit, the usage explorer, insights from your history, local tokens and the provider's colour.
- **History**: 7 or 30 days of daily bars that grow in (click one to open that day), totals, a 90-day calendar, your providers side by side, a busy-hours heatmap, insights and tokens. Days without readings are shown as no data, never as zero.
- **Light and dark**, following Windows or your choice.
- **Motion**: rings settle on springs, percentages roll digit by digit (on the dock too), cards rise in one after another and pages slide. Rings now have comet trails, a hairline for the time left before the reset, a tick for your budget, a soft glow above 90% and a burst of particles when a limit renews. Reduce motion turns the movement off.

**Plan your usage**
- **Budgets** on any limit: "keep Claude's session under 60% by 6 PM". A tick marks it on the ring and bar, and alerts tell you when you're over it or on pace to pass it.
- **Best time to start**: when to start a session so it renews in the middle of your usual busy hours.
- **New alerts**: a limit that would run out within two hours at your pace, a reminder before a busy limit renews, a good moment to start, and a weekly recap on Sunday evening.

**More accounts**
- Watch a second Claude Code or Codex sign-in next to your main one. Each keeps its own folder (CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR / CODEX_HOME), so sign-ins, readings and history never mix. **Add and sign in** opens a terminal for that account only.
- Reorder providers; the order applies to the dock, Overview and your phone.

**Your data**
- **Tokens on this PC**: what Claude Code and Codex used today and this week, the API-price equivalent, 30 days of totals and the top projects and models. Read from the transcripts those tools already keep on this PC; only counts, model names and folder names are stored.
- **Unused capacity, limits reached and reset reliability** for each limit.
- **Incidents** from the Claude, OpenAI and Cursor status pages (can be turned off).
- **Data & privacy**: history size, compact, CSV export, and a fresh read of local tokens.

**Your phone (UsageNotch for Android 1.5)**
- Budgets, colours, names, provider order, alert levels and quiet hours sync both ways.
- Pulling to refresh on the phone now asks this PC to read your providers again.
- The phone gets the 7-day trend, insights, tokens, incidents and extra accounts. Older phone apps keep working unchanged.

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates. Your settings, history and phone pairing are kept.
