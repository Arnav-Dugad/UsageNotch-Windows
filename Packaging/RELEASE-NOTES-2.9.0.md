UsageNotch 2.9 adds one score for all your providers, notes on days, a smarter weekly report, and tokens measured against your limits and your plan.

**Your usage score**
- The top of Overview shows one number, 0–100: the average of how much of each provider's weekly limit you've used (its main limit when it has no weekly one). Beside it are last week's score and each provider's part. It also appears in This week.

**A smarter This week**
- Each provider is compared with your **typical week** (the median of up to 8 earlier weeks) as well as the week before, in one sentence: "You used Claude 20% more than the week before (20% above your typical week)." A ring beside each provider fills to show this week against a typical one.
- **Unusual days** are spotted and named: "Tuesday was about twice your normal Tuesday for Claude." They're marked on the daily chart, and the day card says so.
- When this PC saw less than half the week, a short note says the timing is less certain. Totals are still complete.

**Notes on days**
- Click a day in History and add a one-line note, like "Big refactor" or "Exam week". Notes show as dots on the chart and the calendar, under This week, and on your phone. You can add, change or delete them on either device, and they're kept until you delete them.

**Tokens that mean something**
- **This month at API prices** compared with your plan's price: "$412 at API prices · your Max 5× plan is $100 a month (4.1× its price)".
- **Tokens per session limit**: how many tokens each full limit gave you this week.
- **Model mix**: 8 weeks of tokens by model, as stacked bars.
- **Project budgets**: set a weekly token budget for any project in Settings → Budgets. You'll see its progress under Tokens and get an alert at 80% and when it's passed. Budgets sync with your phone.

**Plan history**
- UsageNotch records when your plan changes, such as Pro → Max 5×. You'll find it in History's All time card and on each provider's page.

**Motion**
- Report rings fill one after another and numbers count up. Switching between 7, 30 and 90 days morphs the chart: days slide and resize into place instead of redrawing. The tray ring glows three times when you cross your warning level.

**Fixes**
- The daily token chart no longer shows a "1 full limit" line and a percentage average, which only make sense for usage limits. Its tooltips now show tokens and cost.
**Phones**
- UsageNotch for Android 1.9 shows the same score, notes, plan history and token details, and edits to notes and project budgets sync both ways ([protocol](https://github.com/Arnav-Dugad/UsageNotch-Windows/blob/main/docs/PHONE-PROTOCOL.md)).

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates.
