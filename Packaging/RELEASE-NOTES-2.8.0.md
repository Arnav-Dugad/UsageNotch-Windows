UsageNotch 2.8 tells you how your week went in plain English, keeps every reading until you delete it, and uses each provider's official logo.

**This week, in plain English**
- History now opens with **This week**: how the last 7 days compare with the 7 before ("You used Claude 35% more than the week before"), limits you reached, your busiest day, the hours you usually work, what your last weekly limit left unused, streaks and tokens.
- Each provider gets a row with this week's total in limits, last week's, and the change.
- **Share your week** makes a story-sized image (1080 × 1920) of your rings, your week and its highlights. It's saved to Pictures › UsageNotch and copied, ready to paste. It never includes account names or e-mail addresses.

**History that's kept**
- Readings stay on this PC until you delete them in Settings → Data.
- History shows **7, 30 or 90 days**, and a new **All time** card shows your total since the first reading, active days, how often you reached a limit and your best day.

**Forecast ranges**
- Pace lines now give the likely window, not just one time: "At this pace: limit around 4:10 PM (likely 3:40–4:50 PM)", or "as early as 3:40 PM" when the slower pace wouldn't reach the limit before it resets.

**Look and feel**
- **Classic style** (Settings → Appearance → Style): solid, grouped cards on a plain background, with no ambient light or glass. It matches Classic on your phone.
- **Live tray ring**: the tray icon shows your first provider's usage as a ring with the percentage inside. Hover it to see every provider. You can turn it off in Settings → Behaviour.
- **Official logos**: Gemini's 2025 multicolour spark and the Codex mark, from the providers' published artwork (Lobe Icons). The OpenAI API keeps the OpenAI mark, and Gemini's default colour is its new blue.
- A short **What's new** sheet appears once after an update (and a welcome on a first install). You can open it again from Settings → Updates.

**More accurate**
- Unused capacity, limits reached and reset reliability now count real limit periods. Before, a sleep gap or a reset time that moved by a minute split one week's limit into several "periods", so averages and counts could be wrong.
- A Codex limit whose reset rolls forward while you aren't using it is no longer counted as a moved reset.

**Fixes**
- Codex model limits showed "Â·" instead of "·" in their names.
- The quick-start guide in the download had garbled punctuation.
- "1×" now reads as "100%", and limit counts read "once", "twice", "3 times".

**Phones**
- Paired phones receive the weekly report and all-time totals, so UsageNotch for Android 1.8 shows exactly the same sentences and numbers. Older phones ignore them.

Installed copies update automatically: the new version installs on next launch, or choose **Restart to update** in Settings → Updates.
